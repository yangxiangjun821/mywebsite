A field guide for paying attention

Vanishing Wild is a student-made introduction to endangered animals, the places they need, and the choices that influence their future.

The purpose

This site turns a large subject into approachable profiles. Each entry asks where an animal lives, what threatens it, and what useful protection looks like.

It is built with semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and small JavaScript interactions that remain understandable to its student author.

Editorial note

Conservation status can change as assessments are updated. This first edition avoids population estimates and keeps its claims introductory.
