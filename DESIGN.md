# Design System

## Direction

Vanishing Wild uses the visual language of an editorial conservation field guide: warm paper, forest surfaces, serif headlines, thin specimen dividers, and an amber signal colour.

## Palette

- Forest: `#183a2e`
- Moss: `#315f45`
- Field-paper background: `#f2f0e7`
- Amber signal: `#d59a3a`
- Ink: `#1e2d25`

## Layout

Content uses a broad 1120px reading frame. The homepage begins with a photographic forest viewport; internal pages favour open reading space and ruled field-guide rows that collapse to one column on smaller screens.

## Interaction

Motion is brief and functional. Controls have visible keyboard focus. Habitat filtering is the primary interaction; the Stubborn Habitat is the playful exercise and always returns to its original state.
