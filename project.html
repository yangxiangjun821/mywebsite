My project idea

Frame & Feeling

A calm digital photography journal that connects each image with the story, mood, and small details behind it.

Talk about the idea →

What I want to build

Frame & Feeling will help photographers slow down and remember more than just the final image. Each entry will combine a photograph with its location, camera settings, weather, and a short reflection.

Capture the context

Save the lens, aperture, shutter speed, and light conditions alongside every photograph.

Keep the feeling

Add a mood, colour, or memory so the image keeps its emotional meaning over time.

Notice patterns

Browse entries by season, place, or mood to discover how my photography changes.

My favourite: Frame & Feeling

A photography journal for beginners to preserve the places, settings, and feelings behind their images.

The first version will be a four-page static website with entries authored in HTML. Accounts, uploads, and a database are outside the initial scope.

The four planned pages

  1. Home — index.html: introduce the journal and highlight a photograph and its story.
  2. Gallery — gallery.html: present photographs with meaningful captions.
  3. Field Notes — notes.html: record locations, light, camera settings, and reflections.
  4. About & Contact — about.html: explain the journal and provide contact details.

These are page names for the future project, rather than links to unfinished coursework pages.
