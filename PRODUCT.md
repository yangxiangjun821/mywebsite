# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and curious beginners seeking a clear introduction to endangered animals and conservation.

## Product Purpose

Vanishing Wild introduces threatened species through short profiles of habitat, pressure, and practical protection.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML, CSS, and plain JavaScript. The site must remain responsive, keyboard accessible, understandable by its student author, and usable through Live Server. Facts should remain introductory, avoid unstable population estimates, and acknowledge that conservation assessments change.

## Brand Commitments

The subject is endangered wildlife. The working name is Vanishing Wild. The voice is attentive, calm, urgent, and hopeful.

## Evidence on Hand

One generated Asian elephant hero image at images/wildlife-hero.jpg. Existing course pages, icons, and student-authored content remain available.

## Product Principles

- Introduce one clear idea at a time.
- Connect every animal to its habitat, pressures, and wider ecosystem.
- Prefer accurate, understandable language over technical density.
- Keep every page usable on a phone and with a keyboard.
